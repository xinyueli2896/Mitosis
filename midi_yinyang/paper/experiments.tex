\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup_exp}

\subsection{Data}
\label{sec:data}

We evaluate on POP909~\cite{pop909}, 909 pop songs with a lead melody
and human chord annotations. The melody track gives stream $x$; stream
$y$ is rendered from the chord annotations as a bass note plus up to
four upper tones. Both streams are quantised to a shared sixteenth-note
grid at 120\,BPM, so a frame is one sixteenth and a bar is 16 frames,
and both are tokenised at a polyphony cap of 8. The cap is not
arbitrary: chord frames peak at five simultaneous onsets and melody
frames at six across the whole corpus, so no frame is clipped.

Songs whose sorted index is a multiple of ten are held out from every
model we train, giving [X] evaluation songs unseen by both the
two-stream training and the single-stream finetunes. Each held-out song
is cropped to a common starting point: we find the first sounding
melody note and keep the bar it falls in plus one before it, so every
system is prompted from the melody's entry rather than from whatever
introduction the song happens to have. Crops are written at a fixed
120\,BPM, so the frame grid is identical across systems.

\subsection{Training}
\label{sec:training}

\sys uses a 12-layer stack of the blocks of
Sec.~\ref{sec:attention}--\ref{sec:permod_moe} at $d = 768$, with
$E = 4$ experts, $k = 2$ active per position, and expert width
$d_f = 4d$. Both backbones are initialised by copying one pretrained
single-stream Transformer into each, as Sec.~\ref{sec:setup} describes;
the cross-stream and within-frame gates start at bias $-10$, so
training begins from that model run independently over the two streams.
Training windows are 384 frames, and the query-token objective
supervises one frame per step, drawn uniformly from the window.
[X: optimiser, schedule, steps, hardware.]

\subsection{Systems compared}
\label{sec:systems}

Every system is prompted with the same crops and generates the same
span, so the comparison is over what the systems do rather than what
they were given.

\textbf{Single-stream.} A CP Transformer over one merged token stream,
where stream identity survives only in each note's program token. We
report it finetuned on POP909 from a large-scale pretrained
checkpoint, and trained on POP909 from scratch. This is the closest
matched opponent: the same tokenisation and budget, without the
two-stream construction.

\textbf{Cascade.} A single-stream specialist continues one stream
alone, and a published conditional model~\cite{yinyang} generates the
partner conditioned on it, in both directions. This is the standard way
to obtain two streams from one-stream machinery, and it is what fixing
a direction in advance costs.

\textbf{External.} Whole-Song-Gen~\cite{whole_song}, a hierarchical
diffusion model that inpaints a whole lead sheet through form,
counterpoint and lead-sheet levels, prompted with the same crops; and
the Anticipatory Music Transformer~\cite{anticipatory}, a
general-purpose model over arrival-time events, which co-generates both
streams but was trained on a different corpus and tokenisation, so it
is reported as an out-of-domain reference rather than a matched
opponent.

\textbf{Ablation.} The same two-stream backbone with strict causal
attention and no query tokens, which isolates what order-free
prediction adds over sequential joint generation.

\subsection{Protocol and metrics}
\label{sec:protocol}

Each system receives a five-bar prompt (80 frames) and generates to 416
frames, 21 bars of continuation, with three samples per song at
temperature $1$. Metrics are computed on the generated span only and
are calibrated against the ground-truth continuation of the same song,
so a system is measured against what that song actually did rather than
against a corpus average.

We report three families. \emph{Coupling}: chord-tone coverage, the
share of generated melody notes whose pitch class lies in the
concurrent generated chord, reported as a delta against the same
statistic on the reference pair. \emph{Distributional fit}: Jensen--
Shannon divergences between generated and reference distributions of
harmonic rhythm, pitch class, note density and duration.
\emph{Survival}: the fraction of frames left empty, which catches the
failure where one stream falls silent and the other continues alone.
A listening test [X] covers what the objective metrics cannot.
